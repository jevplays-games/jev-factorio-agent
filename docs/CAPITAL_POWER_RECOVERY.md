# Capital producer power prerequisites

On 2026-10-05, the continuous campaign placed and configured a copper-cable
assembler, then repeatedly withheld its wood prerequisite as `Candidate evidence
insufficient`. This was separate from the earlier decision-ledger crash.

The retained accepted observation at tick 11220056 binds assembler 2580 and the
paid gather of one wood. The capital continuation supplied an empty dependency
path after construction, so the power evidence could not identify the consumer's
product. Its capital-prefixed ID also differed from a direct power-plan ID. In
addition, the installed observer reported actor inventory headroom only for coal;
the raw-wood prerequisite correctly required a fresh wood capacity reading.
Repairing just one of these conditions would not qualify the action.

At tick 11224265 the capital intent expired through its existing bounded deadline.
The controller retained the paid assembler and exhausted investment failure key,
then resumed other production. That was an observed recovery of other work, not
proof that the assembler had power. Do not extend the deadline, erase its failure
key or replay construction to hide this failure.

## Repair and evidence boundaries

Keep the fresh expansion path when acquiring the producer's construction kit or
power supplies: its eventual product can be an ingredient of the kit or poles.
Bind the existing consumer's catalog-bound product in the power annotation
separately, without introducing a physical recipe cycle. For capital power
evidence, validate the full current specification and recompile its continuation;
require the exact stage, tick, ID, paid step and native consumer annotation.
A capital marker alone grants no permission.

The ordinary planner can reuse a paid producer after capital intent expires.
It also separates recipe-consumer purpose from physical power expansion and
disables optional nested investment while acquiring power supplies. Otherwise
the next cable craft for the poles would encounter a false cycle through the
very assembler being powered. Technology and infrastructure cycle guards remain.

A fixed query now reads insertable counts for coal, wood, iron ore, copper ore
and stone in the same RCON command as the existing observation. It changes no
installed callback or native ownership record. The decoder requires the exact
session, actor, surface, force and tick, normal quality, complete bounded counts,
and agreement with the primary coal reading. Missing, malformed, conflicting or
unsupported optional evidence leaves only the primary coal capacity available.
These counts are independent estimates, not simultaneous inventory reservations;
native actions still check their own immediate preconditions.

## Fast diagnosis and verification

1. Capture the exact rejected request, response and matching accepted observation.
   Check the named consumer's unit and recipe, dependency path, offered step and
   capacity evidence at that same tick. Do not treat a current reading as proof
   of capacity in a historical snapshot.
2. Reproduce the continuation offline with the retained native catalog graph.
   Compare the ID and paid step, then identify the missing witness. Inspect
   `capital_abandoned` events to distinguish a deadline release from repair.
3. Validate malformed identities, stale evidence, altered steps, missing headroom
   and the original rejected JEV answer. Improved evidence must not convert an
   already rejected answer into permission or weaken strict selection.
4. Merge and deploy through the existing signed source and checkpoint procedure
   at a reconciled boundary. Preserve campaign identity, receipts, failed
   investment history and decision budgets. Verify fresh useful native progress
   and the live OBS Program output after resumption.
